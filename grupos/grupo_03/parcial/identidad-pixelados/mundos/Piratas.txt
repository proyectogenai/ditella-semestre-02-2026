a golden age caribbean pirate port stacked up a steep cliff: a crowded harbour
packed hull to hull with tall wooden sailing ships, their masts, rigging, ropes
and torn sails filling the upper half of the frame, stone docks and wooden piers
below crammed with barrels, crates, nets and cannon, a fortress with battlements
and gun ports cut into the rock, tiered rows of colonial houses with tiled roofs
and wooden balconies climbing the slope, taverns, a market, a gallows, a
shipwright's yard, sea caves and flooded tunnels at the waterline, rope bridges
and wooden stairways connecting every level, inhabited by ragged sailors, naval
officers in coats and tricorn hats, dock workers and merchants, its recurring
props and collectible objects: treasure chests, gold coins, skulls, compasses,
lanterns, rum bottles, anchors, cutlasses, parrots, monkeys, tattered flags,
rolled charts and coils of rope, every location, character and object of this
world collapsed together into one impossible plane,
