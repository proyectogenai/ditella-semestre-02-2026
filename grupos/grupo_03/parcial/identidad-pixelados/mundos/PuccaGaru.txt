an east asian village built up the terraced slopes of a misty mountain, drawn in
the flat bold shapes of korean television animation: rows of curved tiled roofs
with upturned eaves, red painted pagodas and gates, paper lanterns strung across
every alley, a noodle restaurant with steaming kitchens, woks and stacked bamboo
steamers at its centre, market stalls, tea houses, a dojo, a bath house, a temple
with stone stairways, bamboo groves, koi ponds and arched footbridges, narrow
stepped lanes and rooftops used as walkways, inhabited by round simplified
characters with large flat eyes and tiny bodies: cooks in aprons, black clad
ninjas leaping between roofs, monks, delivery riders and villagers in bright
tunics, its recurring props and collectible objects: noodle bowls and chopsticks,
red lanterns, folding fans, shuriken, delivery boxes, teapots, cherry blossom
branches, scrolls, bamboo poles, dumplings and firecrackers, every location,
character and object of this world collapsed together into one impossible plane,
