## Prompt final (16:9)

```
Recreate the concept of the "Where's Wally" photo attached as reference, but
set in the Monstrix world. DO NOT copy the illustration literally: borrow
the concept (panoramic view + one hidden character + total chaos) and turn
it into an extreme aerial scene stuffed with life.

CAMERA FRAMING — VERY FAR SHOT:
- High-angle, wide establishing shot taken from far away and above, outside
  the village: the whole geography fits in the frame at once. Think of
  looking down at a model village, not standing inside the street.
- Visible at the same time: the village with its round houses, the frozen
  river, the sledding hill, the forest of plush trees and the market plaza.
  The LANDSCAPE dominates; the monsters are tiny dots.
- MORE THAN 300 figures, seen from above: the crowd reads as a dense, furry
  texture of heads, barely individual. Roofs, stall canopies and tree crowns
  cover most of the image; people only peek out between them.
- NODI is a tiny speck, one dot among hundreds, easy to lose: from this
  distance you need to look carefully to tell the real one apart from the
  trick objects. Its red scarf reads as a tiny red pixel.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES:
- EXACTLY ONE NODI in the entire image. Any other sky-blue + red element
  must be an OBJECT or a PATTERN, never another identical monster. Count
  before finishing: exactly one NODI.
- The scene extends past all four edges of the frame: stalls, sleds, houses
  and people cut off by the margin, as if the world continued sideways,
  above and below.

CHAOS (the mood of the image):
- The scene is a crowd jam with accidents visible from above: snow
  splashing as pale puffs, spilled fruit as colored specks, banners
  waving, steam rising from pots, a runaway balloon, a monster slipping
  with a tower of boxes scattering, another diving out of the stampede.
- Loads of things happening AT ONCE, in every zone, all mid-action: no
  one stands still; everyone is moving or reacting.
- The crowd overlaps itself: clusters hide each other, and many characters
  are only partially visible between heads, roofs, canopies and tree
  crowns. The eye must get lost trying to find someone's full body.
- Mix of scales within the shot: closer, slightly bigger figures still
  trick depth, while the far background is one full wall of tiny crowd.

WALLY RULE (Monstrix atlas):
- True depth: the village horizon at the back, the plaza and hill in the
  middle, a few slightly larger subjects in the foreground hinting scale.
  Figures clearly shrink with distance.
- Identical objects repeated everywhere to confuse the eye: identical
  stalls, identical scarves, identical street lamps, identical snow
  sculptures, identical subjects in the same poses.
- NODI hidden, integrated, with no focus: no glow, no halo, no special
  size. At this distance the game is: separate the one real tiny
  sky-blue+red speck from the hundreds of identical sky-blue+red trick
  objects below.

MICRO-SCENES (readable from above, as clusters; each one a compact zone
with its own colored mess, mid-action):
1. TOP LEFT: the skating rink — a dark ice patch on the snowy ground; the
   crashed skater is a tiny fallen dot with a puff of kicked-up snow around
   it, a few dots spinning, one stain = the collision.
2. TOP CENTER: the sledding hill cuts down the whole side: overturning
   sleds are small dots with snow-spray trails behind them; the pile-up at
   the bottom is a cluster of tiny bodies in the snow.
3. TOP RIGHT: the human pyramid, tumbling: three tiny dots scrambling, one
   of them mid-fall above the others, barely bigger than the houses.
4. CENTER: the market plaza, a dense carpet of fur heads filling the frame
   between stall canopies; two queues wind like fur rivers; from the giant
   cook pot a puff of steam; the hawker on the crate is a raised dot, the
   running monster between legs is a gap closing in the crowd.
5. CENTER RIGHT: the sled race — a torn streak of streamers, a burst of
   snow where the crash happened, the judge's whistle a white flash; the
   stands read as a bumpy ring of heads.
6. CENTER BACKGROUND: the parade snake winding through the street, the
   giant red banner a moving stripe between the houses.
7. BOTTOM LEFT: the snowball battle across a plaza: white puffs bursting
   mid-air over two lines of tiny dots, one fighter's face a small white
   patch.
8. TOP RIGHT BELOW THE HILL: the tipped fruit stall — fruits are colored
   specks spilled across the ground, sliding dots around them, a tiny
   vendor dot chasing.
9. BOTTOM CENTER: the slipping carrier: his boxes scatter like square dots,
   products rolling as colored specks, the dodgers are two small jumps in
   the texture, the last box caught mid-air is a dark little square.
10. BOTTOM RIGHT: the argument: two dots locked close together almost
    touching amid a ring of onlookers; the camera flash is a tiny white
    blink.
11. RIGHT, LAST PLANE: the runaway red balloon — a single red dot rising
    above the rooftops, the dangling monster a smaller dot below it, a
    line of tiny pointing paws under.
12. BACKGROUND: the whole village horizon: roofs almost touching, the
    windmill's plush blades moving over rooftops, puffs of chimney smoke,
    birds as tiny specks, the frozen river crossing between houses.

TEXTURES EVERYWHERE (the most important thing):
- ALL objects carry NODI's fluffy, plushy texture: spongy snow floor,
  plush-wrapped street lamps, trees with round plush tops, plush stall
  roofs, plush merchandise, padded sleds, felt banners. Nothing is smooth
  or cold: everything is plush.
- Even the chaos debris is fuzzy: the whole world seems made of the same
  fur as NODI, so it is impossible to tell character from object at a
  glance. From high above, this fluff gives the scene its scale.

TRICK OBJECTS (reproduce NODI's colors to trigger false positives; NONE of
them is NODI):
1. Rows of street lamps: sky-blue ice #91d3eb plush columns with red light
   globes like ear-flaps: read from afar, they look like a line of NODIS.
2. "Snow cotton" stalls selling sky-blue ice plush balls on red sticks;
   the canopies are giant folded red scarves: piles of them look like
   stacked NODIS.
3. Sky-blue dyed snow sculptures in the flower beds: plump figurines, no
   neck, with a red scarf wrapped and red ear-flaps: NODI imitations
   standing in the street, repeated all over the village.
4. Round trees with sky-blue ice plush canopies dotted with shiny red
   fruit, repeated in rows and in the background: patterns identical to
   the character.
5. A sky-blue ice picnic blanket with red fringe laid out with pieces of
   red plush tart on it: out of the corner of the eye it looks like a
   sitting NODI.

FICHA FÍSICA DE NODI (el único buscado, palabra por palabra):
- Hijo del medio · 12 años
- Pelo celeste hielo #91d3eb que le cubre todo el cuerpo
- Rostro: cabeza grande; dos ojos redondos blancos con pupila negra + tercer
  ojo más chico en el centro, un poco elevado; sin cejas; nariz negra
  redonda; orejas de gato
- Boca: sonrisa con dos colmillos que cuelgan SOLO debajo de la línea de la
  boca; sin manchas ni marcas claras arriba
- Cuerpo regordete, sin cuello
- Vestuario: bufanda roja que le envuelve los hombros + orejeras rojas peludas

MUNDO:
- Mundo invernal de fantasía para monstruos peludos. Toda la escena cubierta
  de textura de felpa suave y mullida. Todo regordete y redondeado, sin
  bordes duros ni puntas.
- Luz: día invernal, blanca y suave.
- Nunca: puntiagudo, terrorífico, duro, oscuro siniestro.

BLOQUE DE ESTILO:
- TÉCNICA: estilo 3D, formas suavizadas, poco puntiagudo, poco realista, peludo
- PALETA: celeste hielo (#91d3eb), blanco nieve y azul, rojo como acento; los personajes aportan su propio color de pelaje
- REFERENCIAS: estilo Pixar, personajes de caricatura redondos estilo kawaii, mundo invernal acogedor, fotografía de animación 3D
- CALIDAD: high quality, textura lisa y limpia, iluminación de animación profesional, no text, no logos

Format: 16:9, landscape, panoramic.
```

## Historial de iteraciones (documentación del proceso)

- v1 — Concepto: subir a Gemini una foto de "¿Dónde está Wally?" y pedirle
  replicarla. Punto de partida: prompt que describe las reglas del mundo
  Wally (densidad, Wally escondido con su camiseta, objetos perdidos, chistes
  visuales) sin copiar la lámina literal (copyright).
- v2 — Cambio de mundo: el mismo concepto pero ambientado en el mundo
  Monstrix, con la ficha de NODI como buscable y la Regla Wally del atlas.
- v3 — Test en Gemini: salieron MUCHOS NODIs. Corrección: un solo NODI,
  contado y verificado antes de terminar.
- v4 — "Too acotado": vista mucho más amplia, más puestos y objetos; todos
  los objetos (piso, faroles, árboles) con la textura felpa de NODI para
  confundir; objetos trampa celeste+rojo inventados.
- v5 — Aún demasiado cerrado: caos máximo, densidad 300+, microescenas en
  simultáneo con accidentes, escena que se corta por los bordes del cuadro.
- v6 — Idioma: descriptivo en inglés (los modelos de imagen rinden mejor),
  anclas del sistema (ficha + mundo + bloque de estilo) en español, palabra
  por palabra.
- v7 — Demasiado primer plano: plano aéreo muy alejado (toma alta desde
  afuera del pueblo); los monstruos son puntitos, la multitud es textura.
- v8 — Ratio: 16:9 apaisado panorámico.
