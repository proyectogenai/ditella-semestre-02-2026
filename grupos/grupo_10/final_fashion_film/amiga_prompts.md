# La amiga — prompts para diseñarla

La amiga es la narradora del libro (la que persigue a Lourdes y siempre llega
un rato tarde). Nunca se dibujó. Este prompt genera su hoja de personaje.

Uso: un chat nuevo de ChatGPT, solo texto, sin imágenes. Copiás el bloque y
reemplazás `[PEGAR ACÁ LA OPCIÓN]` por el texto de la opción elegida.

Las tres opciones son a propósito distintas de Lourdes (sin pañuelo en la
cabeza, sin bob castaño y sin cartera).

## Prompt

```
01 — PURPOSE
A character design sheet of one new character, "the friend", for an illustrated fashion short film. She is a young woman who runs after her best friend from party to party and always arrives a little late and out of breath. The sheet will be used as the reference to draw her again in many scenes, so all views must show exactly the same character with exactly the same outfit.

02 — STYLE
Flat European editorial illustration in the look of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow. Caricature-like face: dot eyes, expressive eyebrows, angular nose. Thin, slightly angular body with natural anatomy and simple clothes with few folds.

03 — PALETTE
Use only these flat matte colors plus the dark ink line: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream. NO pink, NO neon.

04 — CHARACTER
[PEGAR ACÁ LA OPCIÓN]
She also carries a large canvas tote bag with a long strap across her body, open at the top. Her expression is happy but a little out of breath.

05 — COMPOSITION
Three full-body views of the same character side by side, all at the same height, on a perfectly plain pure white background with generous white space around each view: (1) standing, seen from the front; (2) walking, seen in side profile; (3) running, seen in side profile. No ground, no floor, no shadow, no other people, no props other than her bag. Landscape format, 1536x1024px.

06 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO labels. NO logos. NO watermark. NO photorealism. NO gloss or 3D rendering look. NO bob haircut. NO polka-dot headscarf.
```

## Opciones para la sección 04

**A. La corredora**

```
She has long straight black hair tied in a messy ponytail, a white shirt with the sleeves rolled up, wide mustard yellow trousers and flat canvas sneakers.
```

**B. La de rulos**

```
She has big curly burnt orange hair, round glasses, an oversized dusty blue-grey blazer over a warm cream dress, and ankle boots.
```

**C. La de boina**

```
She has short straight blond hair and wears a brick red beret, an open olive green raincoat over a cream top, and striped tights with flat shoes.
```

## Elegida

**Opción C, la de boina.** Ya está generada: `looks/outfit amiga de lourdes.png`
(tres vistas: de frente, caminando y corriendo, las dos últimas mirando a la
derecha).

## Prompt con imagen adjunta: la amiga en más poses

Sirve para generar las poses del encuentro (plano 21 en adelante) sin que
cambie el personaje.

Uso: un chat nuevo de ChatGPT, **con una sola imagen adjunta:
`looks/outfit amiga de lourdes.png`**, y se pega este prompt.

**Archivo:** `amiga_poses_encuentro.png` (guardarlo en `looks/`).

```
01 — PURPOSE
Redraw the character in the attached image for an illustrated fashion short film. The attached image is the reference of "the friend", a young woman who runs after her best friend from party to party and always arrives a little late: copy her face, her short blond hair, her red beret, her outfit, her canvas tote bag, her body proportions and her drawing style exactly from it. She must appear as the same character, shown in six new poses on one sheet, to be used as the reference to draw her again in many scenes.

02 — WHAT STAYS THE SAME (never change these)
Her short straight blond hair. Her brick red beret. Her open olive green raincoat over a cream top, her tights with vertical stripes in mustard yellow and dusty blue-grey, and her black flat shoes. Her large cream canvas tote bag with a long strap across her body, open at the top. Keep her recognizable as the same young woman.

03 — POSES
Six different poses of the same character, each full body: (1) running, seen in side profile facing RIGHT, with a happy and out-of-breath face; (2) bent over with both hands on her knees, catching her breath, seen in side profile facing RIGHT; (3) standing, seen from the front, looking down into her open tote bag; (4) standing, seen from the front, holding one arm out with an open hand as if offering something; (5) standing, seen from the front, with both arms wide open ready for a hug and a big smile; (6) standing, seen from behind, looking away.

04 — STYLE
Redraw her in the flat European editorial style of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. A slim, slightly angular figure with natural anatomy (one head, two arms, two legs, joints in the right places) and a simple caricatured face: tiny dot eyes, expressive eyebrows, a small angular nose and a simple line for the mouth. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow, NO anime look.

05 — PALETTE
Colors only from: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream, plus her blond hair and the dark ink line. NO pink, NO neon.

06 — COMPOSITION
Six full-body views in a grid of three columns and two rows, all at the same height, on a perfectly plain pure white background with generous white space around each view. No ground, no floor, no shadow, no other people, and no props except her tote bag. Landscape format, 1536x1024px.

07 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO labels. NO logos. NO watermark. NO photorealism. NO 3D rendering look. NO bob haircut. NO polka-dot headscarf. NO cup, NO drink in her hands, unless a pose asks for it.
```
