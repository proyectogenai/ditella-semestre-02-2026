# Look de la casa — prompt de Lourdes

Es el look de pijama de Lourdes. Sirve para los planos de la casa: despertar
en la cama (planos 1 y 2) y salir caminando (plano 4). El look de pijama y el
de casa son el mismo. Sale de la imagen base, igual que los otros looks.

Uso: un chat nuevo de ChatGPT, **con una sola imagen adjunta:
`lourdes_base.png`** (está en `parcial/identidad-lourdes/`). El outfit
(pijama mostaza con vivos crema y pantuflas rojo ladrillo) es una propuesta:
se puede cambiar.

```
01 — PURPOSE
Redraw the character in the attached image for an illustrated fashion short film. The attached image is the reference of Lourdes, a young woman who loves parties: copy her face, her hair, her bandana, her body proportions and her drawing style exactly from it. She must appear as the same character in a new outfit, shown in six different poses on one sheet, to be used as the reference to draw her again in many scenes.

02 — WHAT STAYS THE SAME (never change these)
Her short brown bob haircut. A black bandana with small white polka dots worn on her head like a headscarf: a triangular folded cloth covering the top of her head, tied in a small knot at the back of her head with two short pointed tails hanging. Her bob hair shows below it and frames her face. It is NOT a headband, NOT a bow on top of her head, and NOT worn around her neck. Keep her recognizable as the same young woman. In this pajama outfit she carries NO bag at all: do not draw the handbag that appears in the attached image.

03 — WHAT CHANGES
OUTFIT: a cozy pajama set, as if she has just woken up: a mustard yellow short-sleeved button-up pajama shirt with thin warm cream piping along the collar and the edges, matching loose wide-leg long pajama trousers with the same cream piping, and flat slip-on slippers in brick red.
POSES, six different poses of the same character, each full body: (1) standing relaxed, seen from the front, a little sleepy; (2) walking, seen in side profile facing RIGHT, with the arms swinging; (3) standing, seen from the front, with both arms raised to tie the bandana at the back of her head; (4) standing, looking down at a phone in her hand; (5) walking fast, seen in side profile facing RIGHT, leaning slightly forward as if in a hurry, with the arms swinging; (6) stretching with both arms above her head and yawning, seen from the front.

04 — STYLE
Redraw her in the flat European editorial style of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. A slim, slightly angular figure with natural anatomy (one head, two arms, two legs, joints in the right places) and a simple caricatured face: tiny dot eyes, expressive eyebrows, a small angular nose and a simple line for the mouth. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow, NO anime look.

05 — PALETTE
Outfit colors only from: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream, plus the black of the head scarf and her brown hair. NO pink, NO neon.

06 — COMPOSITION
Six full-body views in a grid of three columns and two rows, all at the same height, on a perfectly plain pure white background with generous white space around each view. No ground, no floor, no shadow, no other people, and no props except what each pose needs. Landscape format, 1536x1024px.

07 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO labels. NO logos. NO watermark. NO photorealism. NO 3D rendering look. NO handbag, NO bag, NO purse. NO cup, NO drink in her hands, unless a pose asks for it.
```
