# Prompts de los planos del storyboard

Cada plano es una imagen fija que después se anima en un clip de 3 segundos.
Los 60 planos están en el orden del `storyboard.md`.

## Cómo se usa

- **Un chat nuevo de ChatGPT por plano.**
- **Una sola imagen adjunta**, la que dice cada plano (la hoja de looks de
  Lourdes o la de la amiga). Los planos donde aparecen las dos usan una
  imagen que junta las dos hojas (ver "Referencia del encuentro").
- Se pega el **bloque madre** con el texto del plano en la sección 04. Solo
  cambia la 04, igual que en el parcial.
- Las imágenes tienen que salir **horizontales, en proporción 16:9 y en buena
  calidad** (el bloque madre ya lo pide: 2560 × 1440 px).
- **Chequeá los píxeles de lo que te da ChatGPT** (clic derecho → Obtener
  información, en Mac). Si sale 3:2 (1536 × 1024), pedile en el mismo chat
  "make it 16:9, 2560x1440" o recortalo a 16:9 en Figma.
- **Nombre del archivo:** al lado de cada plano está el nombre con el que hay
  que guardar la imagen (`plano_NN_descripcion.png`). Guardalas en
  `final_fashion_film/storyboard/`.
- Después, pedile a ChatGPT la versión en alta resolución.
- Las caminatas (planos 5, 14, 15, 24, 25, 34, 35, 44 y 45) usan una
  plantilla aparte: ChatGPT dibuja a Lourdes dentro de cada fondo.

## Bloque madre

```
01 — PURPOSE
One single still image: a shot from an illustrated fashion short film. The image will later be animated into a 3-second video clip, so it must have one clear subject, a clear foreground, middle ground and background, and room for natural movement (hair, fabric, wind). Horizontal landscape format in an exact 16:9 aspect ratio (2560x1440px), in high quality and sharp detail, composed for the full frame with no borders or margins.

02 — STYLE
Flat European editorial illustration in the look of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. Caricature-like faces: tiny dot eyes, expressive eyebrows, small angular nose. Slim, slightly angular bodies with natural anatomy. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow, NO anime look. Match the drawing style, line and colors of the attached reference image exactly. If Lourdes appears in the shot, she keeps her short brown bob and wears the black polka-dot bandana on her head like a headscarf, tied in a small knot at the back, never around her neck, unless the shot says otherwise.

03 — PALETTE
Use only these flat matte colors plus the dark ink line: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream, plus the black of the head scarf and the brown of the hair. NO pink, NO neon.

04 — SHOT
[TEXTO DEL PLANO]

05 — CAMERA
Eye-level camera, straight on, never an aerial view, unless the shot says otherwise. Flat color zones for walls, sky and ground so the picture breathes.

06 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO letters. NO numbers. NO logos. NO watermark. NO photorealism. NO 3D rendering look. NO cup, NO drink in anyone's hands, unless the shot asks for it. NO people other than those named or described in the shot.
```

## Plantilla de caminata

Se usa en los planos 5, 14, 15, 24, 25, 34, 35, 44 y 45. La composición es
siempre la misma (mismo tamaño y lugar de Lourdes, cámara fija) para que el
"pum" entre una fiesta y la otra se vea limpio. Cambian la hoja adjunta (el
look) y el fondo.

Lourdes se ve **de lejos, de cuerpo entero y de perfil**, caminando de
izquierda a derecha. La hoja adjunta sirve solo para la cara, la ropa y el
estilo de dibujo: **la pose no se copia de la hoja**, se describe en el texto
(las hojas no tienen una pose de caminata de perfil en todos los looks).

```
A wide, distant walking shot that will be cut together with other walking shots of the same film, so the composition is FIXED. Lourdes, the character in the attached reference sheet, wearing exactly the outfit shown in the sheet, is seen FULL BODY, from the top of her head to her shoes, walking from the left to the RIGHT of the picture, mid-stride, with one leg forward and the other leg back and her arms swinging naturally. She is seen in strict SIDE PROFILE: her whole body and her face are turned 90 degrees away from the camera, her nose and her eyes point to the RIGHT, and we see only one side of her body and one eye. She is NOT facing the camera, NOT in three-quarter view, and NOT looking at the viewer. The camera is far away: her whole figure is small in the frame, about 40% of the frame height, centered horizontally, with her feet at about 75% of the frame height and plenty of empty space above her head and around her. The camera is static and straight on. Use the attached sheet ONLY for her face, hair, bandana, outfit, bag and drawing style, NOT for her pose. [OBJETO] Behind her: [FONDO]. No one in front of her.
```

Si ChatGPT igual la dibuja de frente o muy grande, pedile en el mismo chat:
`She is still too big and facing the camera. Redraw her much smaller (about 40% of the frame height), in strict side profile walking to the right.`
