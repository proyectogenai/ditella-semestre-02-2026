# Misceláneas del libro — prompts para generarlas

Dos láminas de elementos sueltos, en el mismo estilo que las escenas, para
decorar las páginas del libro (tapa, introducción, postales, colofón):

1. **Hoja de objetos sueltos:** 20 objetos de fiesta para recortar.
2. **Viñetas y adornos:** divisores, esquinas, motivos chicos y cintas en
   blanco.

## Cómo usarlos

- Un prompt por chat, en un chat nuevo de ChatGPT, solo texto (sin
  imágenes de referencia).
- Salen sobre fondo blanco liso y sin sombra. En Figma, sacales el fondo
  (Remove background) y recortá cada elemento.
- Pedile a ChatGPT la versión a alta resolución antes de recortar, igual
  que con las escenas.
- Los objetos de la hoja **no repiten** los 8 objetos perdidos de Lourdes
  (zapato, sombrero, pareo, antiparras, llaves, cámara, mate y sombrilla),
  para no confundir la búsqueda del final.
- Si ChatGPT dibuja menos elementos de los pedidos, o los junta, pedile
  otra tanda en un chat nuevo en vez de corregir la lámina.

## Prompt 1 — Hoja de objetos sueltos

```
01 — PURPOSE
A single sheet of loose party objects for an illustrated "search and find" storybook. Each object will be cut out and placed by hand as decoration on the pages of the book, so every object must be drawn separately and read clearly even when shrunk small.

02 — STYLE
Flat European editorial illustration in the look of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow.

03 — PALETTE
Use only these flat matte colors plus the dark ink line: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream. Each object uses two or three of them. NO pink, NO neon.

04 — OBJECTS
Exactly 20 different party objects, each drawn once and each different from the others, arranged in a loose grid of 5 columns and 4 rows:
a champagne bottle with the cork popping, a cocktail glass with a tiny paper umbrella, a plastic party cup, a slice of birthday cake with a candle, a glittering disco ball hanging from a short chain, a string of triangular bunting flags, a bunch of three balloons tied together, a paper cone of confetti spilling over, a party horn blower, a pair of maracas, a tambourine, a small drum with two sticks, a vinyl record in its sleeve, a pair of loudspeakers stacked together, a string of round festoon lights, a roll of paper streamers unrolling, a bowl of popcorn, a slice of lemon, an ice bucket with a bottle inside, a pointed party hat with a pom-pom.

05 — COMPOSITION
All 20 objects on a perfectly plain pure white background, evenly spaced, each one fully separate with generous white space around it so that no two objects touch or overlap. All objects about the same visual size, seen from their most recognizable angle, with simplified shapes and a bold, high-contrast silhouette. No ground, no floor, no shadow, no hands, no people, no labels. Landscape format, 1536x1024px.

06 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO logos. NO watermark. NO real brand names. NO photorealism. NO gloss or 3D rendering look. NO decoration around the objects.
```

## Prompt 2 — Viñetas y adornos

```
01 — PURPOSE
A single sheet of decorative graphic elements for an illustrated "search and find" storybook about parties. Each element will be cut out and placed by hand in the margins and text pages of the book (dividers, corners, small accents), so every element must be drawn separately and read clearly.

02 — STYLE
Flat European editorial illustration in the look of "The Parisianer" (Éditions de la Martinière): thin, dark, slightly irregular hand-inked pen outline (natural hand wobble, NOT perfectly smooth, NOT thick, NOT glossy) and solid flat matte color fills applied as clean shapes inside the lines. NO shading gradients, NO cel-shading highlights, NO gloss, NO painterly texture, NO paper grain, NO cast shadow.

03 — PALETTE
Use only these flat matte colors plus the dark ink line: mustard yellow, brick red, olive/forest green, burnt orange, dusty blue-grey, warm cream. Each element uses one to three of them. NO pink, NO neon.

04 — ELEMENTS
The sheet has four groups, each in its own band, from top to bottom:
Group A, four long thin horizontal dividers, each a different design: a string of triangular bunting flags hanging in a gentle curve, a row of small scalloped awning edges, a line of tiny five-pointed stars alternating with round confetti dots, a garland of round festoon lights.
Group B, four corner ornaments, each a different design, drawn for the top-left corner of a page: a corner made of bunting flags, a corner made of three overlapping balloons, a corner made of a curl of paper streamers, a corner made of a small bunch of leaves and round fruits.
Group C, eight small spot motifs, each different: a five-pointed star, a four-pointed sparkle, a spiral streamer, a small firework burst, a cluster of confetti pieces, a curved arrow, a round loop of ribbon, a small crescent moon.
Group D, three blank ribbon banners with swallowtail ends and two blank rectangular frames with rounded corners and a thin double border, all completely empty inside, with no lettering.

05 — COMPOSITION
All elements on a perfectly plain pure white background, each one fully separate with generous white space around it so that no two elements touch or overlap, and each group clearly apart from the next. Flat front view, no perspective, no ground, no shadow, no people. Landscape format, 1536x1024px.

06 — EXCLUSIONS
NO text. NO letters. NO numbers. NO logos. NO watermark. NO real brand names. NO photorealism. NO gloss or 3D rendering look.
```
